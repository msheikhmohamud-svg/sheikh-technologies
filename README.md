## Official Software Portfolio

### 1. RepFlow — AI Workout & Nutrition Tracking
- **Official Website:** [https://getrepflow.app/](https://getrepflow.app/) ([EN](https://getrepflow.app/en/) · [DE](https://getrepflow.app/de/) · [IT](https://getrepflow.app/it/) · [ES](https://getrepflow.app/es/))
- **Web App:** [https://app.getrepflow.app/](https://app.getrepflow.app/)
- **Support & Legal:** [Support](https://getrepflow.app/support) · [Privacy Policy](https://getrepflow.app/privacy) · [Terms](https://getrepflow.app/terms)
- **Contact:** `contact@getrepflow.app` · `support@getrepflow.app`
- **Summary:** Mobile-first workout, strength progression, and nutrition tracking platform with two specialized AI assistants: **James** (workout programming, lifting loads, progression) and **Jessy** (meals, calories, macronutrients). Supports optional read-only Apple Health (HealthKit) integration on iPhone. Free plan and monthly/annual Premium plans available; fitness & wellness guidance only (not medical advice).

### 2. Sismondi — Swiss Accounting & Personal Finance Software
- **Official Website:** [https://sismondi.app/](https://sismondi.app/)
- **Key Pages:** [Sismondi Personnel](https://sismondi.app/personnel) · [Sismondi Pro](https://sismondi.app/pro) · [FAQ](https://sismondi.app/questions-frequentes) · [Guide : Choisir un logiciel comptable suisse](https://sismondi.app/guide-comptabilite-suisse) · [Legal](https://sismondi.app/informations-legales) · [Privacy](https://sismondi.app/confidentialite)
- **Contact:** `contact@sismondi.app`
- **Summary:** Swiss financial management software structured into **Sismondi Personnel** (4.90 CHF/month or 49 CHF/year — private accounts, budgets, expenses, tax documents) and **Sismondi Pro** (Pro individuel at 24.90 CHF/month or 249 CHF/year for Swiss freelancers; Pro Équipe at 49.90 CHF/month or 499 CHF/year currently in preparation). Web interface accessible; desktop releases in preparation. *Note: Using Sismondi does not replace official filings with the Swiss Federal Tax Administration (AFC/ESTV), and exact operational coverage should be verified prior to production accounting use.*

### 3. Ordyo — Swiss Administrative Software (In Development)
- **Official Website:** [https://ordyo.dev/](https://ordyo.dev/)
- **Privacy:** [https://ordyo.dev/confidentialite](https://ordyo.dev/confidentialite)
- **Contact:** `contact@ordyo.dev`
- **Summary:** Swiss administrative software in preparation for freelancers, SMEs, teams, and organizations, centralizing correspondence & documents, task execution & coordination, and resources/office supplies (*économat*), with optional AI assistance and user-controlled validations. Web version in limited beta; macOS and Windows desktop installers in preparation; *Indépendant* and *Équipe* pricing to be announced.
